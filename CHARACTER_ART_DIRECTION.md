# Subway Thots Hotel — Adult Avatar Art Direction

## Reference synthesis

The provided references establish a polished anime/semi-real character direction:

- Large expressive eyes with strong catchlights and controlled iris gradients.
- Clean facial planes, soft blush, subtle lip color, and confident or playful expressions.
- High-detail hair with layered strands, glossy rim highlights, bangs, ponytails, waves, and color accents.
- A mix of dark brunette/black hair, pastel pink/purple hair, and blonde variants.
- Fashion-led silhouettes: fitted nightlife tops, jackets, dresses, skirts, denim, streetwear, chokers, earrings, necklaces, hair ornaments, and bags.
- Cinematic lighting: neon magenta/cyan hotel lighting, warm amber interior light, cool subway/window light, and soft rim lights.
- Palette anchors: black, plum, wine red, hot pink, electric cyan, violet, cream, denim blue, and warm gold.
- Characters should read as stylish adults first: distinct faces, varied hairstyles, varied body proportions, varied skin tones, and individual fashion identities.

## In-game character rules

- Every avatar is a clearly adult fictional character, 18+.
- Do not use childlike facial proportions, school-age framing, or ambiguous ages.
- No explicit nudity or sexual acts in the normal game world.
- Social interactions are consent-first: invite, accept, decline, block, and report states.
- Fashion can be bold, glamorous, club-oriented, or flirtatious while remaining suitable for the game's normal playable world.
- Keep character identity separate from clothing so players can customize hair, face, makeup, outfit, accessories, and animations independently.

## Avatar construction target

The first authored avatar pipeline should produce:

1. A neutral adult base body with clean topology.
2. Three facial presets with different eye shapes, brows, noses, and jawlines.
3. Six hairstyle families: long dark waves, short black bob, pastel twin-tail, blonde shoulder-length, high ponytail, and curly/wavy variant.
4. Modular outfit slots: top, bottom, dress, jacket, shoes, jewelry, hair accessory, bag.
5. Four expression states: neutral, smile, amused, and confident.
6. Three animation states for the first city slice: idle, walk, and dance.
7. Neon-friendly materials with emissive accents for the hotel/city lighting.

## Character archetype palette

- Hotel Host: black bob, gold jewelry, plum blazer, cyan accent light.
- Club Regular: long dark hair, magenta nightlife outfit, silver accessories.
- Transit Socialite: blonde waves, commuter-inspired fashion, warm subway lighting.
- Pastel Dreamer: pink/purple hair, light blue outfit, flower accessory.
- Streetwear Icon: cropped jacket, denim, sneakers, bold colored hair streak.
- Penthouse VIP: elegant dark outfit, long hair, gold and wine-red accents.

## 3D production notes

- Author in Blender and export GLB/glTF 2.0.
- Keep the avatar body, hair, clothing, and accessories as separate named meshes where practical.
- Use texture atlases and compressed textures for browser delivery.
- Create a low-poly crowd LOD for distant city guests and a higher-detail hero LOD for room/social interactions.
- Keep origins centered at the feet and use a consistent adult-height unit scale.
- Avoid embedding scene lights or cameras in character GLBs; the game controls lighting.
